# Cloudflare methodology and YandexBot metadata — excerpt

Sources:
- https://developers.cloudflare.com/radar/concepts/bot-classes/
- https://radar.cloudflare.com/bots/directory/yandex

Captured: 2026-10-12 UTC

Cloudflare states that Radar bot scores 1 through 29 are classified as **likely automated**, while scores 30 through 99 are **likely human**. Its YandexBot directory labels YandexBot a verified search-engine crawler operated by Yandex and gives the user agent `Mozilla/5.0 (compatible; YandexBot/3.0; +http://yandex.com/bots)`.

Interpretation: a sharp fall in a Yandex-network automated-request series can reflect loss of search crawling or other automation. It is not, without additional attribution, a measurement of political accounts, troll farms, or propaganda content.
