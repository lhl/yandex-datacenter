# Rohtext fact-check — translated research excerpt

Source: https://rohtext.de/news/afd-bots-offline-yandex-angriffen-was-virale
Captured: 2026-10-12 UTC

Rohtext reports that the viral graphic claiming a 30% and 56% drop in AfD-related activity has no reproducible source, query, account list, or measurement definition. The graphic does not identify whether “activity” means posts, replies, likes, active accounts, or server requests.

Its reported short comparison of German-language X posts without retweets, using the same 18-hour windows on Saturday and Sunday, gave:

| Search term | Saturday | Sunday | Change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| AfD | 14,375 | 11,594 | -19.3% |
| CDU | 8,147 | 6,694 | -17.8% |
| SPD | 4,971 | 3,144 | -36.8% |
| Grüne/Grünen | 4,676 | 3,340 | -28.6% |

Rohtext emphasizes that these are keyword matches, not bot counts, and that a weekend comparison without historical controls cannot establish a strike effect. It also notes that AfD daily values did not show a continuous immediate collapse in the short dataset.

Interpretation: useful counterevidence to the viral causal claim, but the underlying counts were supplied by Grok and remain a secondary dataset in this archive.
